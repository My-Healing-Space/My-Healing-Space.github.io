About me

I am a Counselling Psychologist with over 20 years of experience working closely with children, parents, educators and families across diverse settings.

Over the years, I have had the privilege of listening to children who struggle to put their feelings into words, and to parents who care deeply yet feel unsure, worried or exhausted. These experiences have shaped my belief that support needs to be compassionate, respectful and deeply human.

My work focuses on:

  • Children and parenting support
  • Special needs and neurodiversity
  • Mindfulness and emotional regulation
  • Creating awareness around children's mental health and wellbeing

I have worked in educational and support settings, including leadership roles within educational support services, and now practise independently as a counselling consultant.

How I work

My approach is grounded in the belief that behavior is communication, and that healing begins when people feel emotionally safe.

I work in a way that is:

  • Child-centred and developmentally sensitive
  • Supportive of parents and caregivers
  • Strengths-based rather than problem-focused
  • Guided by professional ethics and care

There is no rushing here, and no one-size-fits-all approach. Each individual and family is met where they are, with respect for their unique experiences and needs.

This space may be right for you if…

  • You're concerned about your child's emotions, behaviour or development
  • Parenting feels confusing or overwhelming at times
  • Your child has special needs and you're seeking understanding, respectful guidance
  • You're looking for gentler ways to manage stress and emotional overwhelm
  • Your school or organisation values emotional wellbeing and inclusion

If any of this resonates, you don't need to have it all figured out — reaching out is enough.

Professional Guidelines

All counselling services are offered in line with professional ethical guidelines.

  • • Confidentiality is respected at all times
  • • Online counselling is provided where appropriate
  • • Online services are not suitable for emergency or crisis situations

If immediate support is required, please contact local emergency or crisis services.
